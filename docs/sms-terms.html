SMS Program Terms

This page describes the SMS messaging program operated by Las Vegas Soccer School ("LVSS", "we", "us") for the purpose of delivering player registration links to parents and legal guardians.

What you'll receive

  • Type: One-time transactional SMS containing a personalized link to complete your child's registration.
  • Frequency: Typically one (1) message per family per season. Not recurring; never marketing or promotional.
  • Sender: A toll-free number registered to Las Vegas Soccer School.

How parents opt in

You receive an SMS from LVSS only after providing your mobile number to LVSS staff during registration intake — in person at an event, by phone, or via our online registration form — with explicit verbal or written consent. We do not buy, rent, or share phone numbers from any third party.

Opt-out and help

  • Opt out: Reply STOP to any message. You will not receive further SMS from us.
  • Help: Reply HELP, or email registration@lasvegassoccerschool.org.

Costs

Standard message and data rates may apply. Check your wireless plan for details. LVSS does not charge for messages.

Privacy

Your phone number is used only to deliver the registration link and is not shared with third parties for marketing. See our Privacy Policy for full details on data handling, retention, and your rights.
