SMS Program Terms

This page describes the SMS messaging program operated by Las Vegas Soccer School ("LVSS", "we", "us") for the purpose of delivering player registration links to parents and legal guardians.

What you'll receive

  • Type: Transactional account, registration, and team-operations messages — personalized registration links, event reminders for practices and games, tournament and league participation confirmations, monthly fee notifications, and schedule changes for your child's team.
  • Frequency: Message frequency varies. A typical family receives between one (1) and ten (10) messages per month during an active season, depending on how many children are enrolled and how many events their team has scheduled. Off-season activity is lower.
  • Sender: A toll-free number registered to Las Vegas Soccer School.

How parents opt in

You receive messages from LVSS only after providing your mobile number to LVSS staff during registration intake — in person at an event, by phone, or via our online registration form — and checking the SMS-consent box that reads "By checking this box, you agree to receive messages from Las Vegas Soccer School. Message frequency varies. Reply STOP to unsubscribe. Message and data rates may apply." We do not buy, rent, or share phone numbers from any third party.

Opt-out and help

  • Opt out: Reply STOP to any message to unsubscribe. You will not receive further messages from us.
  • Help: Reply HELP, or email registration@lasvegassoccerschool.org.

Costs

Message and data rates may apply. Check your wireless plan for details. LVSS does not charge for messages.

Privacy

Your phone number is used only to deliver the registration link and is not shared with third parties for marketing. See our Privacy Policy for full details on data handling, retention, and your rights.
